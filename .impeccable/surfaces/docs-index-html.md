Scope: the GitHub Pages landing page, Persuade mode. Visitor: a ranked VALORANT player who already looks players up on tracker sites. Action: download the setup from the latest release. Proof: demo-mode screenshots and the README's facts, nothing invented.

## Direction Contract

THESIS: The page is a printed survey sheet of your next VALORANT match, the kind of map you unfold to see the whole ground before you set out. It refuses the dark gamer landing with a neon accent, feature cards and a glowing hero screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: Pale survey green ground carrying real contour lines in burnt sienna, every fifth one heavier. Ink black double-rule sheet border with grid letters on the edges and a faint magenta grid across the sheet. Water blue is the one action colour. Overpass for type, black and uppercase for the title and heads, Overpass Mono only for grid references, figures and measurements. Screenshots sit in gridded map frames as numbered figures. Text sits on cleared paper insets the way a map clears ground under its labels. No rounded cards, soft shadows or gradients.

STORY: The whole lobby is surveyed before round one. The visitor follows one match from agent select to between games, row by row down the sheet, learns it is free and stays on their PC, and downloads it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A map title block across the top with OVERSEER at full weight, the one line on what it does, and Sheet 2.3.1 with a scale bar and north point. Below it the board screenshot in a gridded map frame, captioned as a figure, and beside it the Key, which is the route through the page with a symbol for each stop, and the download in water blue.

SIGNATURE: The Key is a route. Each stop of the match has its own map symbol, the route line draws in once on load, and the same symbols mark each row's margin and the sticky strip that says where you are on the sheet.

RAISES: Secession, the title block set as a strict ruled table. Night Market, every section a stop with its own key symbol. Weather Sun, one restrained ground colour carrying the whole sheet.

FORM: Survey sheet, candidate 7 of 7, seed 7b2fbc31 reroll 1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished. This build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
