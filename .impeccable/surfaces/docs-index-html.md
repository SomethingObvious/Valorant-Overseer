Scope: the GitHub Pages landing page, Persuade mode. Visitor: a ranked VALORANT player who already looks players up on tracker sites. Action: download the setup from the latest release. Proof: demo-mode screenshots and the README's facts, nothing invented.

## Direction Contract

THESIS: The page is the match programme for your next VALORANT game, the thing you would buy at the gate, laid out in the order a match happens. It refuses the dark gamer landing with a neon accent, feature cards and a glowing hero screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: Saturated ultramarine cover stock owns about half the surface, printed in white and one chrome-yellow second ink. Inner spreads are bright white paper in black and ultramarine. Archivo at three widths: ultra-condensed black mastheads, expanded bold caps for section heads, regular text. Tabular figures in fixed columns. Thick-thin printer's rules, screenshots as numbered plates with captions, no rounded cards, shadows or gradients.

STORY: The whole lobby is filled in before round one. The visitor follows one match from agent select to full time and between games, learns it is free and stays on their PC, and downloads it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A programme cover. A top strip with the issue number 2.3.1, Free and Windows 10 and 11. OVERSEER in ultra-condensed black across the full width, cropped at the right edge. One plain line on what it does, the download as a yellow ticket with a perforated stub, and the board screenshot as the cover plate, large, with a caption.

SIGNATURE: A match clock rides in the margin above the pages and moves from 00:00 agent select to full time as you scroll, stopping at each section's time. Each section prints its own time, so the page reads the same without it.

RAISES: Nixie counter, one warm ink only and tabular figures in fixed columns. Toy catalogue, team sheet spreads packed dense. Curved crease, plates numbered and captioned. Metro tiles, the masthead at poster scale and cropped off the edge. Suminagashi, one continuous stock per spread with no floating cards.

FORM: Matchday programme, candidate 3 of 7, seed 7b2fbc31.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished. This build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
